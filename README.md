# My Favorite Series: Scarlet Heart Ryeo

**Scarlet Heart Ryeo** is a Korean drama about a woman who goes back in time to the Goryeo Dynasty.  
She meets several princes and faces love and heartbreak.

> “My heart will always protect you.”

## Main Characters
- Hae Soo  
- Wang So  
- Wang Wook  

## Why I Like It
1. The story is touching  
2. The characters are interesting  
3. The setting is beautiful
